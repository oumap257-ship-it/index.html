Redefining the Skyline & Spirit of East Africa

Talanta Sports City is not just a stadium—it is a bold statement of Kenya’s ambition. Purpose-built along Jamhuri Grounds in Nairobi, this multi-purpose arena fuses cutting-edge sustainable engineering with rich East African design motifs.

Designed for rapid crowd dispersion, ultra-low energy footprint, and acoustics engineered for maximum atmospheric roar, Talanta represents the pinnacle of modern sports infrastructure on the African continent.

Hybrid Grass Surface

The Central Bowl & Hybrid Pitch

Featuring SISGrass hybrid turf technology combining 95% natural grass with synthetic reinforcement for year-round durability. Steeply banked 360-degree seating ensures uninhibited sightlines from any angle.

  • Sub-surface drainage handling up to 100mm rain/hr
  • Dual 4K LED Giant Video Displays (250m² each)
  • Concert load capacity: 20,000 extra pitch-level standing spectators
Ultra-Luxury Hospitality

Presidential & Corporate Lounges

54 exclusive corporate boxes and 2 Presidential Suites offering panoramic pitch views, fine dining catering, private glass balconies, and subterranean VIP parking access tunnels.

  • Dedicated butler service and Michelin-grade dining
  • Direct elevator access to private underground drop-offs
  • Personalized climate control and acoustic glass insulation
High-Performance Center

Elite Athletic Performance Hub

Adjacent auxiliary grounds featuring two full-sized practice pitches, cryotherapy hydro-pools, biomechanical tracking suites, and recovery lounges designed for national team encampments.

  • FIFA-certified artificial turf auxiliary pitch

  • Integrated Sports Science & Biomechanics Lab

Space for over 300 international accredited journalists, equipped with fiber-optic broadcast lines, simultaneous translation booths, high-capacity press conference auditoriums, and VAR operational rooms.

  • 300-seat press auditorium with satellite uplink ports

Architectural Brilliance

Engineering World-Class Standards

Designed for long-term sustainability, seamless operations, and unforgettable acoustic power.

Solar Canopy Roof

Continuous cantilevered roof lined with high-efficiency photovoltaic solar cells generating up to 3.5 MW of clean energy to power the complex.

Acoustic Engineering

Curved sound-reflecting roof geometry captures fan roar and redirects it back into the bowl while mitigating exterior noise pollution.

Smart Turnstiles

NFC, QR, and facial biometrics enable 60,000 spectators to seamlessly enter and fill the stadium bowl within 45 minutes.

Multi-Mode Rigging

Heavy-duty roof trusses designed to carry 120+ tons of concert lighting, pyrotechnics, and audio equipment for megastars.
